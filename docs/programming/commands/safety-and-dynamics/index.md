# Safety and dynamics commands

The switches for the robot's model-based torques (gravity compensation, the inertia, Coriolis and friction
feedforwards, friction compensation in freedrive), collision detection, the declared payload, the safety zones and
the walls at the joint limits in freedrive.

- **They apply to the whole robot, for every client,** until changed or until the robot software restarts.
- **Some switch a protection off.** Their pages say so in a warning.
