# Diagnostics and service commands

The service functions: a test move of one joint, a joint free to turn by hand, the Hall sensor check, the drives'
gains, the control loop's read order, log levels, telemetry captures and recipes, and the motion filters.

- **For setting up and servicing a robot,** not for production programs.
- **Some move a joint or take its torque away.** Their pages say so in a warning.
