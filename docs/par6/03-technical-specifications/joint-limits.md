# Joint ranges and limits

## Positive directions

Joints are numbered 1 to 6 from the base up; joints 1, 2 and 3 are also the base, shoulder and elbow joints, and
joints 4, 5 and 6 wrist 1, wrist 2 and wrist 3. Each picture shows one joint turned 30° in its positive direction,
in colour, over the pose in grey; the arrow at the joint points the same way. The pictures come from the robot's
model in [robot_descriptions](https://github.com/Source-Robotics/robot_descriptions), whose joint directions
follow the hardware.
