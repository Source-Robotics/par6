## Clothing, hair and eyes

A closing joint catches more than fingers: it can pull in hair, loose sleeves, scarves, lanyards and jewellery. Tie
back long hair and take those off before you work at a powered robot, and keep them out of the arm's reach while it
moves. Wear safety glasses with a pneumatic gripper or vacuum tool, with a sharp or pointed tool, and whenever the
robot can drop or throw a part.
